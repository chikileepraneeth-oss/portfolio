EU Flights Data Engineering Pipeline

Pulls live departures for 10 major European airports, processes them with Spark and models airport performance in BigQuery.

  • Cloud Function ingests AviationStack data for hubs like FRA, MUC, LHR, CDG, AMS and MAD into Cloud Storage
  • A second function triggers a PySpark batch on Dataproc Serverless that loads BigQuery
  • dbt staging and mart models compare live delays (average, median, p90) with historical traffic per airport
